# Contributing to Älyviitta (Smart Auraviitta)

Thank you for your interest in the **Älyviitta** open-hardware project! 

This is a **purely non-profit, open-source philanthropy initiative** aimed at improving road safety and preventing wildlife collisions in sub-arctic regions (Vision Zero).

### How You Can Help:
1. **Engineering Review:** Read our `README.md` and review our physical, optical, power, and RF/mesh assumptions.
2. **Open Issues:** Check the [Issues tab](../../issues) for specific engineering questions (battery physics at -40°C, mesh latency, bayonet mechanics, optical beam focus).
3. **Submit Feedback:** Open a new Issue or start a topic in [Discussions](../../discussions) with your critique, suggestions, or lab test data.

All contributions, feedback, and independent peer-reviews are warmly welcomed and remain part of the public domain.
